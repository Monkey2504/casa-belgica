    What it is

    Your room, your key, your contract.

    Sortir du squat is preparing its first building, Casa Belgica: 50 rooms in Brussels, for people who have just got their papers and are leaving a squat. Not a centre, not a dormitory.

    • Your room, your key

      At least 18 m², with your own shower and your own toilet. An individual contract, in your name. Nobody comes into your home.

    • An address

      A real address in your name — the “domiciliation”. That's what opens the door to CPAS support (the RIS), health insurance, a job. And because you live alone in your room, you get the full rate: €1,340 a month, not €894.

    • Time to breathe

      Three to four years to save, to train, to find a proper home. You leave when you're ready — not when you're evicted.

    Do the sums with us

    €425 more every month.

    With your papers and the RIS at the full rate, you get €1,340 a month. A studio in Brussels costs €725 in rent, plus the deposit. You're left with barely enough to live on — and nothing to put aside.

    At Casa Belgica, you pay €300 all included: heating, water, electricity, internet. The rest is yours.

    Who it's for

    Recent papers, leaving a squat.

    For people who have just been regularised, who are leaving a squat or an occupied building, and who have an income — CPAS support or a wage.

    Ballal ASBL has supported more than 150 undocumented or precarious people in Molenbeek since 2015. Mothers, children, whole families, moved from one squat to another over the years: they are the ones who asked for something other than one more shelter, a stable building, their own. Sortir du squat was born from that request, carried by Ballal since 2023. If you've been to the chaussée de Gand, you already know us.

    Don't have your papers yet? Casa Belgica won't be able to house you — the model relies on CPAS support. But come anyway: Ballal's support is there for you right now.

    Nobody will ask you to pay anything to sign up. Signing up is free, with no conditions, and commits you to nothing.
