What it is

Your room, your key, your contract.

Leave the Squat is preparing its first building, Casa Belgica: 50 rooms in Brussels, for people who have just got their papers and are leaving a squat. Not a centre, not a dormitory.

Your room, your key

At least 18 m², with your own shower and your own toilet. An individual contract, in your name. Nobody comes into your home.

An address

A real address in your name, the official one (the “domiciliation”). That's what opens the door to CPAS support (the RIS), health insurance, a job. And because you live alone in your room, you get the full rate: €1,340 a month, not €894.

Time to breathe

Three to four years to save, to train, to find a proper home. You leave when you're ready, not when you're evicted.

Do the sums with us

€400 to €550 more every month.

With your papers and the RIS at the full rate, you get €1,340 a month. A studio in Brussels costs €700 to €850 in rent, plus the deposit. You're left with barely enough to live on, and nothing to put aside.

At Casa Belgica, you pay €300 all included: heating, water, electricity, internet. The rest is yours.

Who it's for

Recent papers, leaving a squat.

For people who have just been regularised, who are leaving a squat or an occupied building, and who have an income: CPAS support or a wage.

The project is run by Ballal ASBL, in Molenbeek, which has supported more than 150 people since the occupations. If you've been to the chaussée de Gand, you already know us.

Don't have your papers yet? Casa Belgica won't be able to house you: the model relies on CPAS support. But come anyway: Ballal's support is there for you right now.

Nobody will ask you to pay anything to sign up. Signing up is free, with no conditions, and commits you to nothing.

Sign up

Two minutes, three questions.

Before you tick: the front of the building is public, anyone can see your first name and your photo. If you have a reason not to be visible, don't tick: your sign-up counts exactly the same, your window lights up without a name. You can change your mind whenever you want, we remove everything on request.

Your first name, your contact details and your national ID number are kept by Ballal ASBL, only to get back in touch with you and identify you about Casa Belgica. We don't give them to anyone, and we delete them if you ask at admin@ballal.be. Your national ID number is never shown publicly. Your first name and your photo only appear on the front if you tick the box. To have them removed, write to us.
